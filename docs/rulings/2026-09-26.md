### A looping procedural motion is keyable through its settings
> I would be making a procedural thing that loops keyable. How does that work?

**The controller's answer:** the loop runs on its own clock; keys drive its settings over the take (speed, amount, contrast, colour), never its phase directly. Phase is the running sum of speed over time, so a keyed speed change never jumps. Off is amount keyed to 0. A looping export can round the loop period to the clip so the first and last frames match.
