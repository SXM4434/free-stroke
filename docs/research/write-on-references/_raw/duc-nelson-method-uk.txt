# Nelson Handwriting Method

The Nelson Handwriting is the predominant UK handwriting curriculum system for teaching progressive modern cursive writing.

## Teaching Progression by Age

### Preschool to Second Grade (4–7 years)
Students are taught the **unjoined style** with individual letter formation.

### Third and Fourth Grade (7–9 years)
1. First learn **precursive letters with lead-out strokes** (entry/exit formations)
2. Then learn the **semi-joined style**, where:
   - Most lowercase letters are connected
   - Uppercase letters are NOT joined

## Letter Formation Techniques

Nelson teaching methods cover:
- Language of letter formation
- Joins and linking handwriting with phonics and spelling
- Teaching methods and organization
- Teacher modeling of Nelson font to children
- Explicit teaching of letter formation and joins in each session

## The Four Joins (Semi-Cursive)

1. **Joins to letters without ascenders**
2. **Joins to letters with ascenders**
3. **Horizontal joins**
4. **Horizontal joins to letters with ascenders**

## Break Letters (Non-Joining)

Letters that are **not joined from**:
- b, g, j, p, q, x, y, z, s

These break the continuous flow and require pen lifts or connection breaks.

## Stroke Sequences

The system teaches explicit stroke order and direction for each letter, with emphasis on:
- Proper letter formation before joining
- Natural letter formation to enable cursive connection
- Lift points and join entry/exit strokes

## Implementation in Schools

Nelson Handwriting provides:
- Clear, practical framework for whole-school handwriting practice
- Age-appropriate progression of skills
- Alignment with National Curriculum Objectives
- Structured units mapping to curriculum
