THE `duc-*.txt`, `tp-*.txt` AND `pen-*.txt` FILES ARE NOT THE PAGES THEY NAME.
Every one is about 2 KB. A CSS-Tricks article, a PMC paper and the Procreate
handbook are not 2 KB. Opening them shows why: they are the gathering agent's
own SUMMARY of the page, in the agent's own headings and prose, under a header
that says SOURCE_URL and HTTP_OK: yes. The fetch may well have succeeded; the
file is still a paraphrase, and at least one of them (tp-jake-archibald-*)
ends with a section of the agent's own opinion set under the source's name.

Treat every one of them as a POINTER to a URL, never as evidence of what that
URL says. Re-fetch before you quote.
