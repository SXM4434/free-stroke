# Handwriting Timing: Paper vs. Tablet Across Ages

Research comparing handwriting fluency and timing metrics across preschoolers, second graders, and adults.

## Writing Duration (Complete Task)

**Loop Task (loops without dots, on paper):**
- Preschoolers: 11,918.97 ms (≈12 seconds)
- Second graders: 9,548.36 ms (≈9.5 seconds)
- Adults: 4,760.90 ms (≈4.8 seconds)

**"Sun and Waves" Task (on paper):**
- Second graders: 14,753.40 ms writing duration
- Adults: 5,039.54 ms writing duration

## In-Air Time (Pauses Between Strokes)

**Loop Task (paper surface):**
- Preschoolers: 394.29 ms per task (≈0.4 seconds)
- Second graders: 127.50 ms per task
- Adults: 0 ms (continuous writing, no lift pauses)

**Interpretation**: Adults write continuously without pen lifts on simple tasks; younger children show substantial pauses.

## Total Pause Time (Complex Task)

**"Sun and Waves" Task (paper):**
- Second graders: 2,664.79 ms total pause time (≈2.7 seconds in 14.8 second task)
- Adults: 1,550.43 ms total pause time (≈1.6 seconds in 5 second task)

## Movement Fluency: Velocity Inversions (NIV)

Number of Inversions in Velocity reflects automaticity—fewer inversions indicate more fluent movement.

**Loop Task (paper):**
- Preschoolers: 76.94 velocity inversions
- Adults: 34.88 velocity inversions

**Interpretation**: Adults produce half the velocity reversals, indicating smoother, more automatic motor control.

## Tablet vs. Paper Comparison

**Key Finding**: Tablet writing produced:
- Shorter in-air times across all age groups
- Faster velocities across all age groups
- Different surface feedback changes temporal patterns

## Clinical Relevance

This study demonstrates that handwriting fluency dramatically improves from preschool through adulthood:
- Writing speed accelerates (3x faster in adults)
- Pause times decrease dramatically
- Movement becomes more continuous and automatic
